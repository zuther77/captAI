## Implemented
- **Workspace:** one at a time. The frozen capture is a copy of the pixels in a non-activating window at the original screen location, with an outline in the system accent color or a custom color and width (Settings > Capture & Recording > Screenshot border, 0–8 pt). The assistant opens beside it by a tested placement rule (right, left, above or below, then a screen edge; tiny captures go to an edge) at 340 × 400 pt, resizable from 340 to 600 pt wide. Both windows are hidden from other apps' captures and excluded from every later screenshot and Quick Copy.
- **Assistant window:** one semi-transparent Liquid Glass window holding the header, a reading card, the composer and a footer. It can be dragged from any empty area (including to another display) and resized. The context row shows size, time and the save folder.
- **Controls:** Pin (stay on top or behave like a normal window), Hide/Show capture, Copy Text (reuses Quick Copy OCR and its toasts), Close or Esc (restores the app that was frontmost). Clicking either the capture or the assistant raises both. The composer sends on Return and adds a line on Shift+Return; Send is disabled while empty or while a reply streams.
- **Mock analysis:** `AnalysisSource` streams text; `MockAnalysisSource` is the only implementation and is labelled in the banner and footer ("Mock analysis · No model connected", "Local"). The footer opens Settings > Providers.
- A blank capture shows a notice and skips the analysis. A display change closes the workspace.

## Found and fixed during manual testing
